Select Left or Right above the instrument palette to assign its sound and choose the hand controlled by mouse/keyboard. Each hand records to its own labeled loop, with matching boundaries. Silent hands produce no empty loop.

R: start / close a loop. Endings snap to the nearest bar. Overdubs start at the next bar. Tempo locks while tracks exist.

Mouse: hold + drag. Scroll: wave shape. Keyboard: Enter sustains, arrows move, W/S adjust wave, Escape releases. Sliders control finger modulation without a camera.

Snap control needs microphone permission. Use headphones. Sharp noises can also trigger it.

Export: 8 bars of the mix. Sessions are held in this tab.
